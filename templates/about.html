ABOUT US

What is TrustLens?

An AI based information verification system designed for everyday internet users.

Our mission

Scammers now operate like software companies — mass producing phishing pages, fake job offers, edited payment screenshots and "prize" messages faster than ever. TrustLens exists to give regular people the same detection power security teams use, in a simple tool anyone can use.

Every scan produces a transparent 0–100 trust score with the exact reasons behind it. We never show a number without an explanation.

🔒
Privacy first

Scans can be run without an account. Your uploaded images are stored only to display results back to you.

📈
Evidence, not vibes

Scoring is driven by concrete signals: SSL validity, suspicious keywords, metadata forensics, OCR text and more.

How the scoring works

0–44 · Dangerous

Multiple strong scam indicators found. Proceed with extreme caution.

45–69 · Warning

Some risk signals detected. Verify independently before acting.

70–100 · Safe

No strong indicators found. Still, always think before you share.
