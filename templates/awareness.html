The golden rules

1. Never share OTPs.

No legitimate bank or platform ever asks for your OTP.

2. Check before you click.

Hover links, and run suspicious URLs through our website scanner.

3. Real jobs never ask for money.

Any employer requesting fees is almost certainly a scam.
